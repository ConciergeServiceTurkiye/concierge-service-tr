Food & Restaurants

Istanbul's food culture is as varied as the city itself. You can eat quickly between monuments, spend an afternoon around a market or build an entire evening around dinner.

What Is Istanbul Food Like?

Istanbul's food scene moves comfortably between a quick simit and tea on the street, a traditional lokanta lunch, a long meze dinner, seafood by the water and contemporary restaurants. The city's official tourism guide highlights its gastronomic diversity, from breakfast and street food to fine dining and local neighbourhood traditions.

What to Try

Street Food

Döner, simit, balık ekmek, midye dolma, börek and more.

Street Food →

Turkish Breakfast

Cheese, olives, eggs, breads, jams, honey, kaymak and tea. Breakfast can be a slow social meal rather than a quick start.

Meze & Meyhane

Small plates, conversation and a relaxed evening rhythm. Ask what is fresh rather than ordering everything at once.

Seafood

Look toward the Bosphorus, Golden Horn and neighbourhood fish markets for seasonal choices.

Modern Turkish

Contemporary chefs reinterpret Turkish ingredients while keeping familiar flavours at the centre.

Sweet Finish

Baklava, Turkish delight, lokum, sütlü tatlılar, ice cream and Turkish coffee make easy stops between sights.

Where to Explore

Historic Side

  • Sultanahmet for traditional restaurants close to major monuments
  • Eminönü for markets, fish, snacks and quick local bites
  • Balat and Fener for cafés and neighbourhood walks

Modern & Local

  • Karaköy and Galata for contemporary restaurants and cafés
  • Kadıköy and Moda for markets, casual dining and neighbourhood life
  • Beşiktaş and Ortaköy for waterfront dining and lively evenings

Eating With Different Needs

Families, older travellers, vegetarians, adventurous eaters and visitors who prefer familiar flavours can all approach Istanbul food differently. When a dish contains ingredients you need to avoid, ask before ordering. For a special dinner, reserve ahead; for everyday food, some of the best experiences are spontaneous.

Prices, opening hours, menus and restaurant availability change. Use this guide for orientation, then verify the current details of a specific venue before travelling.
