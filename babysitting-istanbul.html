Babysitting & Childcare in Istanbul

Travelling with children changes the way you experience Istanbul. For an evening out, a wedding, a hotel stay or a few hours of private time, professional childcare can make the trip considerably easier.

What Kind of Childcare Do You Need?

Hotel Babysitting

A practical option for parents who want to enjoy dinner, a show or an evening in Istanbul while children remain at the hotel.

Private Residence

Some providers arrange sitters for apartments, villas and private residences. Confirm the exact address and service area before booking.

Events & Weddings

Childcare can be arranged around weddings, conferences and private events, including group requests with advance planning.

Daytime & Evening Care

Short sessions can work well when parents want a few hours for shopping, dining, sightseeing or other plans.

What to Check Before You Book

Ask about the sitter

  • Childcare experience and references
  • First-aid / CPR training
  • Background or identity checks
  • Languages spoken
  • Experience with your child's age
  • Experience with allergies, medication or other special requirements if relevant

Confirm the booking

  • Exact location: hotel, apartment, villa or event venue
  • Start and finish time
  • Number and ages of children
  • Cancellation and minimum booking rules
  • Emergency contact procedure
  • Whether the provider sends the sitter's profile before the booking
For infants or children with medical, developmental or special-care needs, describe those requirements before confirming the booking and make sure the provider can support them.

Babysitting While You Explore Istanbul

Childcare can fit naturally into a family itinerary. Parents may use a sitter for a few hours while visiting a hammam, having dinner, attending a wedding or exploring an area that is less convenient with a tired child. For longer family days, combine childcare with a realistic pace, regular breaks and a clear meeting point.
