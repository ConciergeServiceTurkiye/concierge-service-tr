Choose by Age

0–3 years

Prioritise stroller-friendly parks, shorter outings, aquariums and flexible indoor spaces.

4–6 years

Look for animals, simple interactive museums, playgrounds and shorter attractions.

7–10 years

Theme parks, science and transport museums, adventure parks and interactive experiences become more varied.

11+ & teenagers

Consider bigger rides, adventure activities, sports, interactive venues, shopping and neighbourhood experiences.
