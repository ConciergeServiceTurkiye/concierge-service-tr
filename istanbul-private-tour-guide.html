Istanbul Private Tour Guide

A private tour is less about seeing the maximum number of landmarks and more about choosing the parts of Istanbul that fit your interests, pace and available time.

What Can a Private Tour Include?

A private itinerary can focus on the Historic Peninsula, neighbourhoods such as Galata and Kadıköy, food, architecture, shopping, the Bosphorus or a combination. The route can be adjusted around your interests instead of following a fixed group schedule.

Popular Themes

Historic Istanbul

Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Topkapı Palace, Basilica Cistern and the surrounding historic streets.

Neighbourhoods

Galata, Karaköy, Kadıköy, Moda and the waterfront for a different view of daily Istanbul.

Food & Markets

Street food, breakfast, markets, cafés and local specialities can become the focus of the day.

Bosphorus

Combine waterfront neighbourhoods, ferries, viewpoints or a private boat experience.

How Much Time Do You Need?

Half a day works well for one concentrated area. A full day allows you to combine districts or leave more time for meals and stops. If you only have one day, avoid crossing the city repeatedly just to collect landmarks.

Walking or Chauffeured?

Walking tours are useful in compact historic areas. A chauffeur can reduce travel time when your itinerary covers several districts. They are different components and can be combined depending on the route.
