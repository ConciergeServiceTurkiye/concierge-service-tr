QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Portable Wi-Fi FAQ

Can I connect more than one device?

Yes. The device supports up to 6 connected devices at the same time. Larger groups can request more than one device.

Do I need to change my SIM card?

No. The SIM is built into the portable Wi-Fi device.

Is the airport pickup available at night?

Yes. IST, SAW and TZX pickup and return points are available 24/7.

Can I pick up at one location and return somewhere else?

Yes. Pickup and return locations can be arranged separately, subject to the selected location's return options.

Can I have the device delivered to my hotel?

Yes. Same-day hotel or address delivery is available in Istanbul. Hotel reception can receive the device under the reservation name.

What happens if I lose the device or do not return it?

Unreturned devices and SIM cards are subject to the partner's applicable charges. Missing accessories may also incur charges.

Is the internet truly unlimited?

The service is described as unlimited, subject to a fair-use policy. Excessive or prohibited usage can result in speed reduction, suspension or termination under the applicable terms.

How will I receive the pickup and return instructions?

After your reservation request is confirmed and payment is completed, we will send you the relevant photo instructions showing where to collect and return your device. We can share these instructions via WhatsApp or, if you prefer, by email.
