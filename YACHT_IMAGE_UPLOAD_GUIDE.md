## Photo guidance
- Landscape photos are preferred; hero and yacht card images work best around 16:9 or 4:3.
- Show the full boat in the hero image, with clear lighting and minimal obstructions.
- Use actual interior/deck photos to help guests judge space; avoid heavily edited or misleading images.
- Keep the original files backed up. Optimized WebP copies are preferred for photos; use descriptive filenames exactly as listed.
- If a yacht does not have a real photo for a category, leave that file out. The page is designed to show a placeholder until a photo is added.
- Do not rename a photo to a yacht's filename until the supplier confirms which yacht it depicts.
- Only upload images the supplier has authorised Concierge Service Türkiye to publish.
