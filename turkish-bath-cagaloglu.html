A Historic Experience

Three centuries of Ottoman atmosphere.

Cağaloğlu Hamamı was constructed in 1741 in Istanbul's Eminönü, Alemdar district, as a double public hammam intended to generate revenue for the library of Sultan Mahmud I in Hagia Sophia Mosque. The hammam was begun by Süleyman Ağa and completed by Abdullah Ağa, and it remains in operation today.

Before You Visit

Good to know

  • Cağaloğlu Hamamı has separate male and female sections; female attendants serve female guests and male attendants serve male guests.
  • The hammam states that it does not offer couple-service options.
  • Guests who are pregnant are not permitted to receive a service.
  • Guests with heart disease, diabetes or asthma are advised not to use the bath.
  • The hammam advises against use after recently consuming alcohol or when extremely hungry or full.
  • Children under 6 are not permitted in the hammam. Guests under 18 may experience the hammam with an adult.
  • Guests should inform the hammam upon arrival about allergies or sensitive areas that require attention.
