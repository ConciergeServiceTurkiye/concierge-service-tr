Kadıköy & Moda Guide

Kadıköy and Moda offer a different rhythm from Sultanahmet. Markets, cafés, food, independent shops, waterfront paths and everyday neighbourhood life make the area useful for travellers who want to see how Istanbul feels beyond the classic monuments.

What to Do

Kadıköy Market

Explore food shops, produce, bakeries, fishmongers and small local businesses.

Moda

Walk the side streets and waterfront, stopping for coffee, dessert or a relaxed meal.

Ferry Crossing

The journey from the European side is part of the experience, not just transport.

Street Food

Use the neighbourhood as a base for tasting rather than trying to follow a fixed list.

Shopping

Independent shops, books, records, design and local products add variety to the market.

Evening

Kadıköy and Moda have a strong restaurant, café and nightlife scene.

A Simple Half-Day

  • Ferry to Kadıköy
  • Market and food streets
  • Walk toward Moda
  • Coffee or dessert
  • Waterfront at sunset
  • Return by ferry or continue into the evening

Who Will Enjoy It?

Kadıköy is particularly useful for repeat visitors, food-focused travellers and anyone who wants to combine sightseeing with ordinary city life. It also works well as a flexible afternoon after a morning in the historic centre.
