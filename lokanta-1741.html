MORE THAN A RESTAURANT

Two experiences.
One historic address.

Set within the historic Cağaloğlu Hamamı, Lokanta 1741 brings a contemporary interpretation of Turkish cuisine to one of Istanbul's most atmospheric settings. The restaurant's distinctive location connects the city's culinary culture with the heritage of a landmark Ottoman hammam.

Whether you are planning a special dinner or discovering the historic peninsula, this is an opportunity to experience two sides of Istanbul in one remarkable setting.
