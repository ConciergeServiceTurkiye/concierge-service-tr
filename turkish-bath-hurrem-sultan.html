A Historic Experience

Tradition, architecture and renewal.

Designed by Mimar Sinan and commissioned by Hürrem Sultan in the 16th century, Hürrem Sultan Hamamı stands between Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque. Its restored Ottoman architecture brings the traditional hammam ritual into a refined modern experience.

Before You Visit

Good to know

  • Please arrive approximately 15 minutes before your reservation.
  • No personal items are required for the hammam experience.
  • Guests aged 15 and over may experience hammam and massage with an accompanying adult of the same gender.
  • Guests aged 6–15 may receive the hammam experience but not massage treatments.
  • Children under 6 are not permitted to enter the hammam.
