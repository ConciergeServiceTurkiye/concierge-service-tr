Hotel Overview

The Bank Hotel Istanbul is located on Bankalar Caddesi in Karaköy and occupies a historic former bank building. Its current concept combines preserved architectural character with contemporary interiors and local art. The hotel is positioned for exploring Karaköy, Galata, Beyoğlu and the Historic Peninsula.

The hotel currently has 61 rooms. This figure is intentionally kept off the hotel card and is included here as part of the detailed hotel profile.

History & Design

The hotel occupies a former bank building whose historical character has been retained through restoration. Contemporary interiors by Sinan Kafadar combine with original architectural details, creating a hotel closely tied to Karaköy's financial and cultural history.

The hotel's wine cellar exists as part of the property but is not presented as a public tour or visitor attraction.
