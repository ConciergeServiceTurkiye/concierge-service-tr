The Bank Hotel Istanbul

A historic bank building reimagined as a contemporary design hotel.

Hotel Overview

The Bank Hotel Istanbul is located on Bankalar Caddesi (formerly Voyvoda Street) in Karaköy. The historic property combines the Sümerbank Building and the former Cemaathan Building and preserves architectural elements from its banking history.

The building was designed in 1867 by Antoine Tedeschi in the Neo-Renaissance style and later restored by architect Han Tümertekin. Contemporary interiors are associated with designer Sinan Kafadar, while the hotel also presents a curated local art collection.

The Bank Hotel Istanbul has 61 rooms. The property combines its historic architecture with contemporary hospitality, design and guest services.

History & Design

Bankalar Caddesi was formerly known as Voyvoda Street and became a major finance and commerce axis of the Ottoman period. The hotel history document states that the Sümerbank Building was constructed in 1867 by Italian architect Antoine Tedeschi and later hosted Crédit Général Ottoman, Deutsche Bank and Sümerbank.

The hotel building combines the Sümerbank Building with the former community center of the Neve Shalom congregation. Restoration was carried out by architect Han Tümertekin with the stated principle of renewing the building while preserving its original state and artistic value.

The property also retains historical bank details such as vault doors, calculators and cash registers, while local artworks are displayed throughout the hotel.

The hotel has a wine cellar. It is recorded here as part of the property rather than as a public tour or visitor attraction.
