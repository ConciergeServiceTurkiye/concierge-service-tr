Turkish Breakfast in Istanbul

Turkish breakfast is more than a single dish. It is a table of small flavours designed to be shared slowly, usually with tea and plenty of conversation.

What Is on the Table?

Cheese

Several regional styles can appear, from mild white cheeses to stronger varieties.

Olives

Green and black olives are a standard part of many breakfast tables.

Eggs

Menemen, boiled eggs or other preparations can be part of the meal.

Bread

Fresh bread is used to combine the different flavours on the table.

Honey & Kaymak

A classic sweet and creamy combination for a slower breakfast.

Tea

Turkish tea usually stays on the table throughout the meal.

Breakfast by Neighbourhood

European Side

  • Beşiktaş and surrounding neighbourhoods
  • Karaköy and Galata
  • Ortaköy and Bosphorus areas
  • Historic Peninsula options

Asian Side

  • Kadıköy
  • Moda
  • Üsküdar
  • Kuzguncuk

How Long Should Breakfast Take?

If you are visiting Istanbul for the first time, allow at least enough time to sit down rather than treating breakfast as a takeaway meal. For a special breakfast, the setting can be as important as the menu.

Dietary Needs

Turkish breakfast is naturally rich in vegetarian-friendly foods, but individual dishes can contain dairy, eggs, nuts, gluten or meat products. If you have allergies or dietary restrictions, ask the restaurant about ingredients before ordering.
