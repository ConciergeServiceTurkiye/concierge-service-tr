HISTORIC SHOPPING

Grand Bazaar Guide

The Grand Bazaar is one of Istanbul's most recognisable shopping experiences. It is best approached as a historic district of covered streets and specialist shops rather than a conventional mall.

What You Can Find

Carpets & Kilims

Ask about material, origin, age and documentation before making a significant purchase.

Jewellery

For valuable pieces, compare sellers and request clear details and a receipt.

Ceramics

Look for handmade pieces and ask about safe packaging for travel.

Leather & Textiles

Compare quality, material and construction instead of buying on the first offer.

Lamps & Crafts

Colourful traditional objects can make memorable souvenirs, but check packing requirements.

Nearby Markets

Continue toward the Spice Bazaar and Eminönü for food, spices and everyday market life.

How Bargaining Works

Bargaining is part of the culture in many bazaar settings, but it does not mean every price has to become a contest. Know what you are willing to pay, compare a few shops and be prepared to walk away politely if the price or product is not right.

Buying Something Valuable?

  • Compare several sellers.
  • Ask for a written receipt.
  • Confirm material and specifications.
  • Ask about return or exchange conditions.
  • For antiques or valuable goods, check current export rules before purchasing.

Make It Part of a Day

The Grand Bazaar works naturally with Sultanahmet, Spice Bazaar, Eminönü and the historic peninsula. If shopping is your priority, allow enough time for comparison instead of squeezing it into the final 20 minutes of a sightseeing tour.
