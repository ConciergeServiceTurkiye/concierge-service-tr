Water & Outdoor Experiences

There is another Istanbul beyond museums and historic streets: rowing and kayaking on the Golden Horn, cycling routes, island days, green spaces and Black Sea beaches.

Haliç: Rowing & Kayaking

The Golden Horn can be experienced from the water as well as from its waterfront. Rowing and kayaking are among the active ways to explore this side of Istanbul. Availability, weather, safety rules and operator requirements should always be checked before booking.

Cycling in Istanbul

For visitors who prefer to move under their own power, waterfront routes can be an enjoyable way to see parts of the city at a slower pace. Cycling on the Bosphorus and along the city's recreational waterfronts should be planned around traffic, weather, route conditions and local rules.

Princes' Islands

The islands are a natural fit for a slower day away from the busiest central streets. Ferry travel, walking and cycling can be combined depending on the island, season and current local arrangements.

Beykoz & Green Istanbul

Beykoz and the wider northern Bosphorus offer a different landscape from the historic centre, with wooded areas, waterfront views and places suited to a slower outdoor day.

Black Sea Beaches

Istanbul also has a Black Sea coastline. For swimming, use designated beaches and check current sea conditions, water quality, weather and any local warnings before entering the water. The area around the northern Bosphorus and the Third Bridge is not itself a blanket recommendation for swimming; the specific beach matters.

Beach conditions can change. Treat current official beach and safety information as the final reference on the day of your visit.
