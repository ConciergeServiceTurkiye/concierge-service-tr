Getting Around

Public transport is a major part of Istanbul life. For exact routes, service changes and live information, check the official transport source before travelling.

WalkBest for compact neighbourhoods and historic streets.
Metro & TramUseful for crossing busy parts of the city efficiently.
FerryA practical connection and one of the most enjoyable ways to see the water.
Taxi / TransferUseful when luggage, timing or door-to-door travel matters.
