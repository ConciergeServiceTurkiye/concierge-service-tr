FIRST, WHAT IS IT?

What Is Meet & Greet?

Meet & Greet is personal airport assistance. Instead of finding your way through a large airport alone, a representative meets you at the appropriate point and guides you through the relevant airport journey.

You don't buy a person. You buy time, clarity and peace of mind.

At Istanbul Airport, the service can cover an arrival, departure or connecting-flight journey. Depending on the service purchased, assistance can include greeting, luggage support, transfer procedures and access to airport privileges such as Fast Track, Priority Check-In, Lounge or Private Buggy.

Those benefits are not identical for every booking. The exact inclusions depend on the selected service type, so we will confirm what is included before your reservation is finalized.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Meet & Greet FAQ

Does Meet & Greet include a private car?

No. Meet & Greet and transportation are separate services. If you need a car after arrival, we can arrange an Airport Transfer or a Private Chauffeur Service.

Will someone really be waiting for me?

For the iGA service, after the booking is completed, an arriving passenger's flight details are tracked and a personal agent greets the passenger with a sign bearing their name when they enter the terminal.

What if I have a connecting flight?

For a connecting flight, iGA states that the passenger is greeted with a name sign and accompanied toward the iGA Lounge or the boarding gate depending on the departure time.

Do I have to walk through the airport myself?

You still move through the airport, but the purpose of personal assistance is that you are guided through the relevant process rather than having to work it out alone. The exact assistance depends on the purchased service.

Can I request wheelchair or other special assistance?

Yes, special requirements should be stated during booking. iGA specifically gives examples such as wheelchair and sign-language staff.

Can I book less than 24 hours before my flight?

iGA says payment must be made at least 24 hours before flight time. If less than 24 hours remain, you should contact the service provider first so operational availability can be checked.

Is the flower welcome included?

No. iGA states that flower service is not included in Meet & Greet and is subject to an additional charge.
