NATURE & SEA

Parks & Outdoors

When the city feels busy, Istanbul has another side: trees, water, islands, long promenades and quiet corners.

When Istanbul Needs a Slower Pace

Between historic streets and busy avenues, Istanbul has parks, groves, forests, waterfront paths, beaches and islands. Official tourism guidance lists green spaces across both sides of the city and along the Sea of Marmara and Black Sea.

Places to Breathe

Gülhane Park

A historic green space beside the old city and a natural pause during a Sultanahmet day.

Yıldız Park

Wooded paths and historic atmosphere in the Beşiktaş area.

Emirgan Park

A large Bosphorus-side park known for gardens and seasonal colour.

Belgrad Forest

A much larger nature escape for walking and outdoor time.

Fenerbahçe Park

Waterfront paths, greenery and views toward the Marmara and Princes' Islands.

Princes' Islands

Ferries, historic houses, sea views and a slower island rhythm. Büyükada and Heybeliada are popular choices for a day away from the mainland.

Beykoz & the Asian Green Belt

Beykoz offers a different side of Istanbul, with waterfront scenery, green spaces and outdoor areas including Beykoz's parks and recreation grounds.

Eyüpsultan & Pierre Loti

Combine the historic Eyüpsultan area with Pierre Loti Hill for Golden Horn views, a slower walk and an easy change of pace from the historic peninsula.

Balat & Fener

Explore colourful streets, historic buildings and the Golden Horn waterfront on a neighbourhood walk through Fener and Balat.

Istanbul Aquarium

A family-friendly indoor option in Florya when you want to combine an aquarium visit with the waterfront and a break from the historic centre.

Beykoz Millet Bahçesi

A green-space option on the Asian side. Check current municipal information before visiting because facilities and access can change.

Princes' Islands

The main islands regularly served by ferry include Büyükada, Heybeliada, Burgazada and Kınalıada. Official tourism information describes them as popular day trips with preserved historic character and opportunities for walking and cycling.

Ferry schedules and operating conditions can change. Check the current timetable before setting out, especially if you have a return flight or another fixed appointment.

More Outdoor & Family Ideas

For families, a day can combine an easy waterfront walk with Istanbul Aquarium. For a quieter Asian-side day, Beykoz can be paired with a Bosphorus stop. Pierre Loti and Eyüpsultan work well when you want views and history without spending the entire day in Sultanahmet.

Outdoor Ideas by Traveller

  • Picnic-friendly green spaces

Couples & Solo Travellers

  • Bosphorus viewpoints
