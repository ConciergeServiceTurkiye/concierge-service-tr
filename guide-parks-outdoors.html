NATURE & SEA

Parks & Outdoors

When the city feels busy, Istanbul has another side: trees, water, islands, long promenades and quiet corners.

When Istanbul Needs a Slower Pace

Between historic streets and busy avenues, Istanbul has parks, groves, forests, waterfront paths, beaches and islands. Official tourism guidance lists green spaces across both sides of the city and along the Sea of Marmara and Black Sea.

Places to Breathe

Gülhane Park

A historic green space beside the old city and a natural pause during a Sultanahmet day.

Yıldız Park

Wooded paths and historic atmosphere in the Beşiktaş area.

Emirgan Park

A large Bosphorus-side park known for gardens and seasonal colour.

Belgrad Forest

A much larger nature escape for walking and outdoor time.

Fenerbahçe Park

Waterfront paths, greenery and views toward the Marmara and Princes' Islands.

Princes' Islands

Ferries, historic houses, sea views and a slower island rhythm.

Princes' Islands

The main islands regularly served by ferry include Büyükada, Heybeliada, Burgazada and Kınalıada. Official tourism information describes them as popular day trips with preserved historic character and opportunities for walking and cycling.

Ferry schedules and operating conditions can change. Check the current timetable before setting out, especially if you have a return flight or another fixed appointment.
