Shopping

Shopping in Istanbul can be historic, modern, local, luxurious or wonderfully ordinary. The key is knowing which neighbourhood fits what you are looking for.

Shopping in Istanbul

Shopping in Istanbul ranges from centuries-old bazaars to modern boulevards, designer boutiques, local markets and major shopping centres. The city's official tourism guidance describes the Grand Bazaar, modern retail districts and neighbourhood shopping as very different but equally important parts of the experience.

Know the Main Shopping Areas

Grand Bazaar

A historic covered market with jewellery, carpets, textiles, leather, ceramics and souvenirs.

Spice Bazaar

Tea, spices, dried fruit, sweets and food gifts in the heart of Eminönü.

İstiklal & Beyoğlu

A mix of international brands, local shops, cafés, passages and cultural venues.

Nişantaşı

Upscale fashion, design and contemporary shopping.

Kadıköy & Moda

Local shops, markets, books, records, food and independent businesses.

Bağdat Caddesi

A long Asian-side shopping and lifestyle boulevard with boutiques, cafés and restaurants.

Grand Bazaar: What to Expect

The Grand Bazaar is a maze rather than a conventional shopping centre. Official tourism information describes its historic covered streets, many gates, specialist sections and a strong tradition of bargaining. Take your time, compare before buying and never feel obliged to purchase simply because a conversation was friendly.

For valuable jewellery, carpets or antiques, ask for documentation, receipts and clear information about materials, provenance and export requirements where relevant.
