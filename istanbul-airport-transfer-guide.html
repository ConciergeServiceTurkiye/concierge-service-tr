ARRIVAL IN ISTANBUL

Istanbul Airport Transfer Guide

Getting from Istanbul Airport to your hotel is one of the first practical decisions of your trip. The right option depends on your arrival time, luggage, group size, hotel location and how much planning you want to do after a long flight.

Which Airport Are You Using?

Istanbul Airport (IST) is on the European side and is the main international gateway for many visitors. Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW) is on the Asian side. Check your airport carefully before choosing a route, especially when comparing transfer times or arranging a pickup.

Your Main Options

Private Transfer

A driver meets you at the airport and takes you directly to your accommodation. It can be useful when you have luggage, children, a late arrival or simply want a door-to-door journey.

Taxi

Taxis are available, but the final fare and journey time can vary with traffic, route and demand.

Metro & Public Transport

Istanbul has rail and bus connections from the airports. Public transport can be practical for light luggage and travellers comfortable navigating connections.

When a Private Transfer Makes Sense

Consider a private transfer if you arrive very late, travel with several suitcases, have young children, need to reach a hotel with several connections, or want your first journey in Istanbul to be arranged before you land.

What to Confirm Before Booking

Confirm the airport, flight number, arrival date and time, passenger count, luggage requirements and exact hotel or accommodation address. If your flight changes, the operator should also have a clear process for updating the pickup.
