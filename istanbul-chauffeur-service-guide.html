Istanbul Chauffeur Service Guide

A chauffeur service is different from simply taking a taxi: the vehicle and driver are arranged around your schedule. It can be useful for airport arrivals, business meetings, shopping, sightseeing or special occasions.

Chauffeur vs Taxi

A taxi is usually an on-demand point-to-point ride. A chauffeur arrangement is planned in advance around a defined period, route or schedule. The right option depends on how much flexibility and coordination your day requires.

Common Uses

  • Airport pickup and departure
  • Several meetings across Istanbul
  • Full-day sightseeing
  • Shopping or event transportation
  • Special occasions and VIP travel

Choosing a Vehicle

Think about passenger count, luggage, comfort and the amount of time you will spend in the vehicle. A larger group may need a van rather than a sedan, while a business traveller may prioritize a premium executive car.

What to Confirm

Ask about hourly or full-day minimums, pickup location, waiting time, included mileage or route limits, vehicle capacity and what happens if your schedule changes.
