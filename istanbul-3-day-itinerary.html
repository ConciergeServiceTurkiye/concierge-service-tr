3-Day Istanbul Itinerary

Three days is enough to experience several sides of Istanbul without treating every hour like a race. This route keeps the historic centre, European-side neighbourhoods, Asian side and Bosphorus in the same trip.

Day 1 — Historic Istanbul

Morning

  • Hagia Sophia area
  • Blue Mosque and Sultanahmet Square
  • Breakfast or coffee nearby

Afternoon

  • Topkapı Palace or Basilica Cistern
  • Grand Bazaar
  • Dinner around the historic centre or Eminönü

Day 2 — Galata, Karaköy & the Bosphorus

Start around Galata and walk downhill toward Karaköy. Take time for cafés, galleries and the waterfront rather than rushing between landmarks. In the afternoon, choose a ferry or Bosphorus experience. Finish with dinner around Karaköy, Galata or another neighbourhood that suits your evening.

A Bosphorus experience can be public or private. The right choice depends on your group size, timing, privacy preference and budget.

Day 3 — Asian Side & Local Istanbul

Cross the Bosphorus to Kadıköy and continue toward Moda. Explore the market, cafés and side streets, then consider Üsküdar for waterfront views back toward the European side. If you prefer shopping, use part of the day for a planned shopping route instead.

Where to Put a Hammam

A hammam works particularly well after a busy sightseeing day. Instead of adding another monument to your schedule, it gives you a defined period to slow down. Book ahead when timing matters.
