A CLEAR PROCESS

A request is the beginning,
not a confirmed booking.

Concierge Service Türkiye is your point of contact for discussing and coordinating arrangements. We work with relevant service providers to check availability, requirements and the details of each request.

Because services are delivered by different providers, availability, payment arrangements, deposits, cancellation terms and confirmation steps can vary. We explain the applicable details before you decide whether to proceed.

FROM ENQUIRY TO CONFIRMATION

Five steps.
No guesswork.

We keep the process straightforward and make the status of your request clear as it moves forward.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    Send your dates, group details, preferences and any important requirements through the relevant request form or contact channel.

  2. 02

    We review the request

    We check the information you have shared and may contact you if an essential detail is missing.

  3. 03

    We check with the provider

    We contact the relevant provider to review availability, suitability, pricing, inclusions and applicable conditions.

  4. 04

    You review the details

    We share the available option and explain the price, payment or deposit requirements, cancellation terms and next steps where applicable.

  5. 05

    Confirmation is arranged

    If you choose to proceed, we coordinate the next step with the provider. Your booking is confirmed only when the provider's required confirmation process is complete.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful
answers.

01

Is my request a booking?

No. Sending a request does not reserve a service or guarantee availability. We will tell you when the provider has confirmed it.

02

Who provides the service?

The relevant third-party provider delivers the requested service. We help communicate and coordinate the arrangement with you.

03

How does payment work?

Payment arrangements depend on the service and provider. Any required payment, deposit, recipient and timing will be explained before you confirm.

04

Can I make a tailored request?

Yes. Share what you have in mind and we will review whether a suitable option can be arranged, subject to feasibility and availability.

05

How will you contact me?

Use the preferred contact method offered on the relevant form, or tell us whether WhatsApp or email works best.

06

Can details change?

Availability and provider conditions can change until confirmation. We will communicate any relevant update before you proceed.
